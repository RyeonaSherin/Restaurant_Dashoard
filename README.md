# 🍽️ Restaurant Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Title

**Restaurant Analytics Dashboard**

## 📝 Short Description

The Restaurant Analytics Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project that helps restaurant owners monitor sales, customer behavior, and operational performance. It provides visual insights into revenue, popular menu items, customer preferences, order trends, and payment methods, enabling data-driven decision-making to improve profitability and customer satisfaction..

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- 📊 Power BI
- 📑 Microsoft Excel
- 🗄️ SQL (Optional)
- 📈 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- 🔄 Power Query

## 📂 Data Source

The dashboard uses a restaurant sales dataset (CSV/Excel) containing:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer Name/ID
- Menu Item
- Category
- Quantity
- Price
- Total Sales
- Payment Method
- Restaurant Branch (if applicable)

## ✨ Features

- 📈 Total Sales Overview
- 🍕 Best-Selling Menu Items
- 📅 Daily & Monthly Sales Trends
- 💰 Revenue Analysis
- 🍽️ Category-wise Sales
- 💳 Payment Method Distribution
- 🛒 Order Quantity Analysis
- ⭐ Customer Ordering Trends
- 📍 Branch Performance Analysis
- 🔍 Interactive Filters and Slicers

## 📌 Business Problem

Restaurant owners often struggle to identify sales trends, customer preferences, and menu performance. Without proper data analysis, it becomes difficult to optimize inventory, improve customer satisfaction, and increase overall profitability.

## ❓ Key Questions

- What is the total revenue generated?
- Which menu items are the best sellers?
- Which food category generates the highest sales?
- What are the busiest days and months?
- Which payment method is used the most?
- Which branch performs the best?
- What is the average order value?
- Which menu items generate the least revenue?

## 📈 Business Impact & Insights

### Business Impact

- Helps optimize menu offerings based on customer demand.
- Improves inventory planning and reduces food waste.
- Identifies peak business hours for better staff scheduling.
- Supports pricing and promotional strategies.
- Enables data-driven business decisions to increase revenue.

### Key Insights

- Fast food items generate the highest sales.
- Weekend orders are significantly higher than weekdays.
- Digital payments are the most preferred payment method.
- The top five menu items contribute to a major share of total revenue.
- Beverage sales increase during evening hours.
- Certain menu items have consistently low sales and may require promotional offers or menu updates.
